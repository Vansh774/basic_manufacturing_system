========================================================================
         MANUFACTURING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - ANDROID APK GUIDE
========================================================================

File: ManufacturingSystem.apk
App Version: 1.0.0
Architecture: Universal (All Android phones & tablets running Android 5.0 - 14+)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW TO INSTALL ON YOUR ANDROID DEVICE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy "ManufacturingSystem.apk" to your Android phone via:
   - USB cable transfer
   - WhatsApp / Telegram / Email
   - Google Drive / Cloud storage
2. On your Android device, tap on "ManufacturingSystem.apk".
3. If prompted:
   - Enable "Install unknown apps" / "Allow from this source".
4. Tap "Install" and then "Open".

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO CONNECT THE APK TO YOUR FACTORY SERVER
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Make sure your Android phone and your PC/Server are connected to 
   the SAME Wi-Fi or Local Area Network (LAN).
2. Find your PC's IP address:
   - On Windows PC: Open CMD and type: ipconfig
     Look for "IPv4 Address", e.g., 192.168.1.105
3. Open the "Manufacturing System" app on your Android device.
4. Tap the Settings icon ⚙ in the top-right corner.
5. Enter your server URL:
   Example: http://192.168.1.105:5000
6. Tap "Connect".
7. The app connects to your live system in real-time!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. REAL-TIME AUDIBLE ALERTS ON MOBILE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- When logged in as Manager or Owner:
  If any machine is marked as "Stopped" by an operator with a reason,
  the app will automatically play the warning chime/beep sound and
  show a real-time red toast alert banner!
- Note: Make sure your phone's media volume is turned on.
